# Aurvin logo generation

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 8 October 2026, with transparency enabled.
The source logo is saved in `frontend/public/aurvin-logo.png`. The 32px PNG favicon
and the ICO containing 16px, 32px, and 48px images were downscaled from that logo
using Windows System.Drawing, without changing the design.

## Final prompt

```text
+Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Aurvin AI chat application logo, also used at 16px and 32px as a browser favicon.
Primary request: Create one polished, distinctive app icon for Aurvin, a calm, capable personal AI assistant. One bold custom geometric capital A monogram subtly integrated with a speech bubble silhouette, expressing helpful conversation. Keep the A immediately readable.
Style/medium: Minimal flat graphic logo, crisp vector-like edges, timeless and understated.
Color palette: Warm ivory white mark on solid deep charcoal (#202020), matching a neutral grayscale chat interface.
Composition/framing: Square canvas. A single charcoal rounded-square app tile fills almost the whole canvas, centered. The white monogram occupies approximately 70% of tile width. Compact substantial strokes and open counters, readable at tiny favicon sizes. Rounded corners only outside the tile; transparent outside those corners.
Text: Only the single custom A monogram; no wordmark, lettering, or slogan.
Constraints: One icon only. Actual transparent canvas outside the charcoal tile. No sheet of alternatives, mockup, scene, fine lines, tiny accent details, drop shadow, gradients, metallic effects, 3D, watermark, or other brands.
```
